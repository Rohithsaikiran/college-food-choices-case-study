Comprehensive Analysis and Dietary Strategies with Tableau: A College Food Choices Case Study

Making informed food choices is vital for college students' health, academic performance, and overall well-being. This case study analyzes student habits using interactive visualizations to design effective dietary interventions.

Nutritional Preferences

Understanding what factors drive students' food choices—ranging from caloric awareness, fitness goals, taste preferences, to ethical and cultural values.

Behavioral & Lifestyle Factors

Analyzing how student stress, cooking habits, eating out frequency, and daily routines directly affect their nutritional choices and eating behaviors.

Dietary Strategies

Formulating actionable recommendations for university dining halls and individual students to foster healthier dietary habits and nutrition-rich options.
